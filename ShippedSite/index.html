Compliance & data handling

Flying over an active site is a licensed activity.

Here is exactly what that means, and exactly what happens to what we capture.

FAA Part 107

Flowstate operates under 14 CFR Part 107 as a certificated Remote Pilot. Flights are planned inside Part 107 rules — daylight operations, visual line of sight, and controlled-airspace authorization through LAANC where the site requires it.

We do not operate beyond visual line of sight or over people, and we do not claim waivers we do not hold. If a site needs an operation outside Part 107, we will tell you before you book.

Coordinated with your super

We agree a flight window with your superintendent, check in before we launch, and stay clear of active lifts, crane radii and occupied work areas. A typical site is captured in under an hour with no work stoppage.

We need a safe launch point, site access, and notice of any known hazards or restricted areas. That is the whole ask.

Your imagery stays yours

You own the imagery and everything derived from it — orthomosaics, models, measurements, reports. Flowstate holds only the licence needed to process, host and deliver your reports to you.

We never publish or use a client site in marketing without written permission, and we treat site imagery as confidential. Unreleased developments and site security layouts do not belong in a portfolio.

How captures are stored

Reports are served from unguessable links, are excluded from search engine indexing, and can be revoked by you at any time. Anyone holding the link can view the report, so share it the way you would share a document.

Raw captures are retained for 24 months so we can rebuild or re-process a report, then deleted. You can request earlier deletion in writing at any point.
