01Services and deliverables

Flowstate provides aerial imagery capture by small unmanned aircraft, photogrammetric processing of that imagery, and delivery of hosted web reports. What is included depends on the package purchased.

Flight Report — one-time
One capture flight of the agreed site area, one processed orthomosaic, one hosted report link, and a PDF export. Delivered within two business days of a completed flight unless otherwise agreed.
Draw Ready — recurring, three-month minimum
Recurring capture flights at the agreed cadence (monthly or biweekly), each producing an orthomosaic and updating a hosted progress timeline; volumetric measurements where site conditions permit; unlimited sharing of the report link.
Site Intelligence — recurring, three-site minimum
Draw Ready services applied to three or more sites, plus a consolidated portfolio view across those sites and priority scheduling.

Volumetric measurement (cut/fill, stockpile) depends on terrain, vegetation, ground visibility and the availability of suitable reference surfaces. It is provided where site conditions permit and is not guaranteed on every capture or every site.

Services not described above — including boundary surveys, ALTA/NSPS surveys, construction staking, design deliverables, thermal or LiDAR capture, indoor capture, and any operation requiring an FAA waiver we do not hold — are outside the scope of these Terms.

02Scheduling, weather and airspace

Flights are scheduled in advance and are subject to weather, wind, visibility, daylight, airspace availability and site conditions. Flowstate will not fly when conditions would make the flight unsafe, unlawful, or would produce unusable imagery, and is the sole judge of that determination.

Where a scheduled flight cannot proceed, we will reschedule to the next suitable window at no additional charge. Recurring-plan captures missed for weather or airspace reasons are rescheduled within the same billing period where possible; where that is not possible, the capture carries into the following period. Weather and airspace delays are not grounds for a refund or a fee reduction.

If a flight is prevented by a Client-side cause — site inaccessible, no safe launch point, access denied on arrival, or cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice — Flowstate may charge a mobilization fee equal to fifty percent of the capture fee.

07Accuracy and intended use

Flowstate deliverables are not a land survey. Orthomosaics, elevation models, measurements and volume figures are photogrammetric estimates produced for construction progress documentation and reporting. They are not prepared by, or under the supervision of, a licensed land surveyor or professional engineer.

They must not be used for boundary determination, easement or encroachment analysis, legal descriptions, design of record, construction staking, or any purpose for which a survey by a licensed professional is required by law, contract, or lender requirement.

Stated accuracies, including RTK-derived positioning, describe the capability of the equipment under favourable conditions. Achieved accuracy on any given capture depends on satellite conditions, correction availability, terrain, vegetation, ground control, weather and site obstructions, and may be lower.

You are responsible for verifying any quantity, measurement or percentage-of-completion figure before relying on it — including in a payment application, draw request, change order, or any submission to a lender, owner, surety or public body. Flowstate reports are supporting documentation. They are not a certification of completion or of quantities in place.
