01Information we collect

Information you give us

When you submit the contact form or email us, we collect the information you provide: name, company, email address, phone number, site address or city, approximate acreage, which package you are interested in, and anything you write in the notes field. All of it is used to quote and schedule work.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs — IP address, browser user agent, pages requested, timestamps, and referring page. These are used for security, abuse prevention and diagnosing faults.

Information from engagement

If you become a client, we hold the records needed to run the engagement: billing contact and address, invoices and payment records, scheduling correspondence, and site access details you give us.

We do not collect payment card numbers on this site. We do not buy personal information from data brokers, and we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

02Site imagery and capture data

Aerial capture produces imagery of your job site along with flight logs, GPS positions and derived products such as orthomosaics, elevation models and measurements. This material can reveal site layout, construction progress, equipment locations and security-relevant detail, so we treat it as confidential client information rather than ordinary business data.

  • You own it. Ownership of captured imagery and derived products vests in you under our Terms of Service. We hold only the licence needed to process, host and deliver your reports.
  • We do not publish it. Your site is never used in marketing, case studies, portfolio or social media without your prior written permission.
  • Reports are unlisted. Hosted reports are served from long, randomly generated links and are excluded from search engine indexing. Anyone holding the link can view the report, so control who you send it to. You can ask us to revoke a link at any time.

03How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries, prepare quotes and schedule captures.
  • To plan flights, including checking airspace and travel distance for the site address you give us.
  • To process imagery and produce, host and deliver your reports.
  • To invoice, collect payment and keep accounting and tax records.
  • To provide support, re-process a report, or investigate a defect you report.
  • To secure our systems, prevent abuse and comply with legal obligations, including aviation record-keeping.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not use client site imagery to develop or train third-party products.

06How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become work
Deleted within 24 months of the last contact.
Raw captures and processing files
Retained 24 months from capture so a report can be rebuilt or re-processed, then deleted.
Delivered reports
Hosted for the duration of the engagement and 12 months after it ends.
Invoices and financial records
Retained as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically seven years.
Server logs
Retained for the short period our hosting provider keeps them, typically under 90 days.

You can ask us to delete captures or reports earlier than these periods. We will confirm deletion within 30 days, other than records we are legally required to keep.

07How we protect it

Imagery and reports are stored with encryption at rest, transmitted over encrypted connections, and access is limited to the people who need it to deliver the work. Report links are long and randomly generated so they cannot be guessed, and are excluded from search indexing.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information or your site imagery, we will notify you without undue delay and provide what we know about the scope and our response.

08Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate.
  • Delete your information, or specific captures and reports.
  • Provide a copy of your imagery and derived products in a usable format.
  • Revoke a report link.
  • Withdraw permission previously given for portfolio or marketing use.

Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws — including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah and Virginia — have rights to access, correct, delete and obtain a portable copy of personal information, and to appeal a refused request. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

To make a request, email us using the address in Section 12. We may need to verify your identity before acting, and will respond within 45 days.

09Incidental capture of people and property

Aerial capture of a job site may incidentally include workers, vehicles, and neighbouring property visible from the air. We do not capture imagery for the purpose of identifying or monitoring individuals, we do not operate directly over people, and we do not use captured imagery for any form of surveillance of personnel.

If you or a neighbouring owner have a concern about imagery of a specific area or person, contact us and we will discuss masking that area in delivered products or excluding it from future captures.

10Children

This site and our services are directed to businesses, not to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
