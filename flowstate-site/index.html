Core philosophy

Three forces, one system.

Fr

Friction

Complexity belongs behind the scenes. Every system we build gives people an effortless interaction on the surface, whatever it takes underneath.

Mo

Momentum

Interfaces should carry people forward, not stop them at decision points. Motion communicates flow — it never distracts from it.

Pr

Precision

Typography, spacing, timing, architecture — every detail is intentional. Nothing ships because it was easy; it ships because it was right.

The signature experience

Ink suspended in water. Always moving, always seeking equilibrium — responsive without ever being controlled.

The field above isn't decoration — it's a live physics simulation running on your GPU. Velocity, pressure, and dye transport are solved every frame, the same way real fluid settles after being disturbed. Your cursor is an external force, not a controller. The color you see — green rising to teal — is the field's own state made visible: motion and magnitude, not a palette painted on top.

It's a proof of concept for how Flowstate approaches every system we build: behavior should emerge from good architecture, not be scripted frame by frame.
